How to Install

On Android Your device
  1. Tap the Install App button above.
  2. Your browser shows a small “Install app” confirmation — tap Install.
  3. PAC Media lands on your home screen like any app. Open it from there — not from the browser tab.
On iPhone / iPad Your device
  1. Tap the Share button in Safari — the square with the arrow pointing up.
  2. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top corner. PAC Media appears on your home screen.
On desktop Your device
  1. Click the Install App button above.
  2. Confirm Install in the dialog that appears.
  3. PAC Media opens in its own window — find it later in your taskbar or dock.

About this app

PAC Media brings together the full family of magazines published by Pigeon Advertising & Communications in one free app — travel, tourism, property, lifestyle, transport and business titles, side by side.

Browse the shelves, open an issue and read it fullscreen with a realistic flipbook, exactly like the printed edition. New issues appear the moment they are published, and the app keeps itself up to date automatically.

Install it directly from this page — no app store, nothing to sideload. The PAC Media icon lands on your home screen and opens like any other app.
